# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who rely on PantherClaw to keep their systems safe. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

**Enforcement:** report conduct issues privately to the maintainer via the contact details on [@katocxl](https://github.com/katocxl). Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
